FAQ

Questions from companions

Everything you need to know before you apply.

How do companions get paid?

Companions are paid for each trip they accept and complete. Rates are being finalized and will be shared during onboarding.

What are the requirements?

You must be 18 or older, complete identity verification and a background check, and be comfortable using a smartphone app. Most of all, we look for people who are kind, patient and reliable. No travel industry experience is required.

When does AidHandy launch?

We plan to launch in Spring 2027 at Atlanta (ATL), New York (JFK) and Los Angeles (LAX). Companions who apply now are reviewed and onboarded first.

Do I need to book extra flights?

No. You're matched with travelers on flights you've already booked, for work or personal travel. You only accept the trips that fit your plans, and there are no shifts.

Do I need medical training?

No. Companions provide friendly, non-medical travel support only: finding the gate, boarding, settling in and good company. They don't provide medical care, personal care or lifting assistance.

Is this a job or contract work?

Companions work with AidHandy as independent contractors, so you decide which trips to take.

Does it cost anything to apply?

No. Applying is free and there is no commitment. It takes about 2 minutes.

What happens after I apply?

We review your application, complete identity verification and a background check, and walk you through onboarding. We'll email you with launch updates and next steps.
