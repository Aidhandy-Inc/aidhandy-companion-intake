1. Program name and description

The AidHandy messaging program sends transactional and service messages to people who use AidHandy, the platform that connects air travelers with travel companions who are already booked on the same flight. Messages may include:

  • Account security: one-time verification codes for sign-up, sign-in and account changes.
  • Booking and match updates: companion requests, confirmations, changes and cancellations.
  • Trip and flight alerts: reminders before your trip (for example, about six hours before departure) and updates when a flight changes.
  • Account and safety notices: important notices about your account, verification or a trip.
  • Private-number messages: messages between a traveler and their companion, relayed through an AidHandy private number (see Section 5).

AidHandy does not send marketing or promotional text messages. If we ever offer marketing texts, we will ask for your separate, express consent first, and agreeing will never be a condition of using AidHandy or buying any service.

2. How you opt in

You opt in to AidHandy text messages when you add your mobile number in the AidHandy app or website (for example, during sign-up or in your account settings), agree to receive text messages by checking the consent box (which is never pre-checked), and confirm your number with the one-time code we text you. By opting in, you confirm that you are the account holder or an authorized user of that mobile number, and you agree to receive the messages described in these Messaging Terms.

Some features depend on text messages, such as phone verification and private-number messaging during a trip. If you choose not to receive texts, or you opt out later, those features may not be available to you.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your account activity, your bookings and your trips. For example, you may receive a verification code when you sign in, updates when a booking changes, and reminders before a trip. Private-number messages depend on how many messages you and your companion exchange.

4. Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier's standard rates apply to messages you send and receive. Check your mobile plan for details. AidHandy does not charge you for text messages.

5. Private-number messages between travelers and companions

When a booking is confirmed, AidHandy may connect a traveler and their companion through an AidHandy private number, so they can text each other without sharing their personal phone numbers.

  • The private number works only for a limited time around your trip. After that period ends, messages sent to it will not be delivered.
  • Messages through a private number are written by the other user, not by AidHandy. AidHandy does not control or endorse their content.
  • To help keep everyone safe and to support customer service and the resolution of disputes, AidHandy may keep records of private-number messages.
  • Keep messages respectful and related to your trip. Do not send content that is unlawful, harassing, hateful, sexually explicit or violent, or that promotes illegal substances, firearms or other prohibited content. Breaking these rules may lead to suspension of your account under our Terms of Service.
  • Do not share passwords, payment card numbers or other sensitive information by text.

6. How to opt out

You can stop receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP to any AidHandy message. You can also reply STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT. We will send you one final message confirming that you have opted out, and we will not send further messages to that number unless you opt in again.

AidHandy may text you from more than one number, including private numbers assigned for a trip. Replying STOP opts you out of messages from the number you reply to. To stop all AidHandy text messages, reply STOP to each AidHandy number that texts you, or email support@aidhandy.com and we will opt you out of all AidHandy text messages.

To start receiving messages again, reply START or UNSTOP to the number you opted out of, or opt in again in your account settings.

7. Help

For help, reply HELP (or INFO) to any AidHandy message, or contact us at support@aidhandy.com.

Text messages are not for emergencies. AidHandy text messages and private-number messages are not monitored for emergencies. In an emergency, call 911. At the airport or on board, contact airport or airline staff.
