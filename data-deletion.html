Delete your AidHandy account

You can delete your AidHandy account at any time. Here is how.

Option 1: Delete it in the app

  1. Open the AidHandy app and go to Profile.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm the deletion.

Option 2: Ask us by email

Email privacy@aidhandy.com from the email address on your AidHandy account, and say you want your account deleted. We will confirm the request with you before we act on it.

What happens after you ask

After we verify your request, we anonymize your personal information within 90 days. Some records are kept for longer where the law or our policies require it. These include financial, payout, safety, dispute, audit and other legally required records.

If you signed in with Facebook

You can remove AidHandy from your Facebook account under Settings, then Apps and Websites. This stops further sharing from Facebook, but it does not delete your AidHandy account. To delete your account, use one of the options above.

Questions? Contact us at support@aidhandy.com. For how we handle your personal information, read our Privacy Policy.
